## Examination overview and intended audience

NSE5_SSE_AD-26 is the Fortinet NSE 5 - FortiSASE and SD-WAN 26 Core Administrator exam. It evaluates deployment, configuration, daily operations, supported-product integration, incident analysis, troubleshooting, and security-log analysis.

The verified examination information includes:

- Duration: 65 minutes.
- Length: 30–35 questions.
- Scoring: pass or fail.
- Languages: English and Japanese.
- Product scope: FortiSASE 26, FortiOS 7.6, FortiClient 7.0, FortiAuthenticator 6.5, and FortiManager 7.6.
- A score report through the Pearson VUE account.

Use the version 26 scope when preparing. A retirement notice applying to a different examination version should not be transferred to this assessment.

## Keep all five official domains in the plan

Decentralized SD-WAN has a 20–30% range. Review basic architectures, DIA, members and zones, underlay and overlay links, performance SLAs, monitoring, and member state.

Rules and routing has a 15–25% range. Study rule matching and strategies, application steering, member selection, SLA maps, routing principles, static and probe routes, sessions, and introductory centralized management.

SASE deployment carries 20–30%. It includes architecture, provisioning, residency, sovereignty, licensing, onboarding methods, authentication, and integration with SD-WAN.

SIA and SSA represents 15–25%. Review content inspection, profiles and groups, CASB, filtering, endpoint profiles, and compliance.

Analytics has a 15–25% range. It covers rule and session monitoring, FortiSASE threat-related logs, dashboards, and reports.

These are official intervals, not fixed allocations. Do not replace them with midpoints or convert them into promised question counts.
